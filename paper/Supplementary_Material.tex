\section{CPSC2018 preprocessing}
CPSC2018 waveforms were resampled from 500 Hz to 100 Hz with polyphase anti-aliasing using \texttt{scipy.signal.resample\_poly(x, 1, 5, axis=1)}. No additional $\mu$V-to-mV factor was applied because the released arrays used in this study were already mV-scale (range approximately $-4$ to $+3$). Records shorter than 1,000 samples were zero-padded; longer records were center-cropped to 1,000 samples. PTB-XL arrays were used at 100 Hz. Per-lead z-score normalization was applied except for the mV-statistics branch, which receives the 36-dimensional global voltage descriptor before normalization.

\section{Cross-center details}
C0 is trained only on PTB-XL training labels and applied to each CPSC fold without target labels or target statistics. C2/C3 use unlabeled CPSC fold features; they are transductive adaptation, not pure zero-shot transfer. Temperature $T=2.507$ is fitted on the PTB-XL validation split. Few-shot experiments use only Fold~0 training labels and test Fold~0 records. The public CPSC folds provide filenames but no patient identifiers; only fold-level separation can be asserted.

C0 fold AUCs: 0.9080, 0.9092, 0.9091, 0.9071, 0.9185 (mean $0.9104 \pm 0.0041$). The same-fold Fold~0 comparison is Old A0 0.8603 vs. C0 0.9080 ($+4.77$ pp).

\section{Uncertainty and confidence intervals}
TMC clean test uncertainty predicts sample-level error with AUROC 0.738 (95\% bootstrap CI [0.717, 0.760]); error at 10\% coverage is 5.1\%, and at full coverage 38.2\%. The A8-OneWay contradiction rate at seed 42 is 0.79\% (95\% bootstrap CI [0.46\%, 1.16\%]). The counterfactual CSR is 7.12 (95\% CI [4.20, 24.38]) in probability space and 8.70 (95\% CI [6.01, 15.99]) in logit space. ECG-QA full Set EM is 59.83\% (95\% Wilson CI [58.62\%, 61.03\%]).

\section{Data and code availability}
All datasets are public and de-identified: PTB-XL, CPSC2018, and ECG-QA. The ECG-QA train/test split has zero patient-ID overlap (9,100 vs. 2,444 patients). Code, split definitions, and evaluation scripts will be released in a public repository upon acceptance; an anonymized review link will be provided in Editorial Manager.
